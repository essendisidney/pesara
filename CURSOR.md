# PESARA LIMITED — MASTER CURSOR CONTEXT

Read this file before any product or code change.

## What we are building

Pesara Limited is a technology venture studio headquartered in Kenya.

It is NOT a traditional software agency, a loan app, a wallet, or the previous rider companion.

Proposition:

**You bring the idea. We bring the technology. We build the business together.**

Tagline: Ideas deserve execution.

Hero: You have the idea. We have the technology. Let's build the company.

Mission: To ensure great ideas don't die because someone couldn't build them.

## Product philosophy

Pesara isn't selling code. Pesara is building companies.

The founder experience should feel like applying to a serious venture-building institution.

Do not invent production data, fake metrics, or ownership claims.

Do not overbuild. Follow V1 phases in this order:

1. Foundation — design system, public site, auth, database
2. Idea pipeline — submit wizard, founder dashboard, tracking
3. Operating system — admin, viability, committee
4. Venture management
5. Intelligence (AI assists humans; never auto-accepts)

## Brand

Primary CTA: Submit Your Idea

Secondary CTA: How Pesara Works

Do not look like a generic IT consultancy. No stock programmer photos, no circuit boards, no fake digital-transformation copy.

Visual: intelligence, capital, ambition, restraint. Between a VC firm, a product studio, and an investment platform.
