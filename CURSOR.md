
# PESARA LIMITED — MASTER CURSOR CONTEXT

## What we are building

Pesara Limited is a technology venture studio headquartered in Kenya. It co-builds companies with founders.

It is NOT a traditional software agency, a loan app, a wallet, or the previous rider companion.

Proposition:

**You bring the idea. We bring the technology. We get paid when you get paid.**

Hero: You bring the idea. We bring the technology. We get paid when you get paid.

Mission: To make sure great ideas don't die because someone couldn't afford to build them.

## The co-build model

- The founder brings the idea, the market access and the time to run the business.
- Pesara brings product, engineering, and the payment rails (Pesara Rails).
- In a co-build the founder pays no build invoice. Pesara is paid three ways:
  - Equity (typical 15–35%), vesting over 4 years.
  - Revenue share on gross revenue (typical 5–10%) until Pesara recovers 2–3x the build cost, then stepping down (typical 1–3%).
  - Platform fee on the venture's own revenue collected through Pesara Rails (typical 0.5–1.5%). Never on client money the venture holds for its customers.
- The shares are applied at settlement, not invoiced. Until a venture runs on Pesara Rails, shares are settled monthly from statements.
- Pesara backs ideas where money moves through the product. Other ideas go to Pesara Digital (paid builds, no equity, no revenue share).
- Each venture is its own company; the founder keeps the majority. The shared platform stays with Pesara and is licensed to the venture. The founder sees every payment and every split. A buyout clause lets a venture leave the platform.

The single source of these numbers is `web/src/config/partnership.ts`. Change them there, never inline in pages.

## Product philosophy

Pesara isn't selling code. Pesara is building companies it shares in.

Evidence before engineering. The founder experience should feel like applying to a serious venture-building institution.

Do not invent production data, fake metrics, or ownership claims. Pesara Rails components stay marked "Developing" until they run in production. Worked examples are labelled illustrative.

Do not overbuild. Follow V1 phases in this order:

1. Foundation — design system, public site, auth, database
2. Idea pipeline — submit wizard, founder dashboard, tracking
3. Operating system — admin, viability, committee
4. Venture management
5. Pesara Rails — collections, settlement and automatic splits
6. Intelligence (AI assists humans; never auto-accepts)
